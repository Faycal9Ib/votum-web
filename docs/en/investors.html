Investors

Votum was born with the vision of building a new generation of biotechnology solutions for health, human performance and advanced wellbeing.

Company profile

We operate under a vertically integrated innovation model: research, molecular design, galenic development and functional validation are part of a single scientific ecosystem.

This structure speeds up the transfer of knowledge into scalable, high-value solutions with international potential in healthtech, pharma, sport performance and wellness.

Beyond products, we are building a biotechnology infrastructure focused on generating intellectual property, clinical data and new technologies for precision health.
